## 1. Commensuratio (Proportion & Order)

Every element on the ID card is anchored to a strict mathematical grid. By respecting proportional harmony, the design creates an immediate subconscious sense of trust, balance, and institutional authority.

## 2. Ordinatio (Hierarchy & Focus)

We strip away decorative clutter and background noise. The layout establishes an absolute visual hierarchy, ensuring that critical credentials—such as identity and role—are communicated instantly without ambiguity.

## 3. Eurythmia (Graceful Legibility)

Typography and data placement are structured for rapid visual flow. Security personnel and digital scanners can parse the necessary information in a fraction of a second, eliminating any friction during everyday verification.

## 4. Utilitas (Functional Rigor)

Form strictly follows necessity. Engineered with precise print parameters and high-contrast readability, the card remains durable, clear, and functional across both physical manufacturing and modern security workflows.
